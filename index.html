Mission

The TrustSec AI Lab advances research at the intersection of AI and cybersecurity, building AI-driven solutions that enhance the security, robustness, and trustworthiness of interconnected digital systems.

Foundational Work

Network Security

Threat detection across complex distributed environments.

Data Leakage

Prevent sensitive data exposure and model inversion.

Adversarial AI

Defense against adversarial attacks and vulnerabilities.

Large Language Models

Secure deployment and risk mitigation for LLMs.

Research Focus

Adversarial Robustness

Protect AI systems from attacks like fraud and intrusion.

IoT Privacy in Health

Enhance privacy using generative and differential models.

LLM Hallucination

Reduce hallucinations with secure RAG pipelines.

Why It Matters

AI powers decision-making across sectors. The TrustSec AI Lab ensures these systems remain reliable, robust, and trustworthy.
